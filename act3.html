About This Food

Sinigang na baboy is a classic Filipino sour and savory soup featuring tender pork simmered in a tangy broth. Typically flavored with tamarind, it is loaded with vegetables like okra, eggplant, yardlong beans, taro (which thickens the broth), water spinach, and long green peppers, and is always served with steamed rice.

Benefits

  • Muscle Repair & Satiety. Pork acts as a complete protein source, aiding in cell repair and keeping you full. To manage fat intake, you can use leaner cuts like pork loin or shoulder (kasim) instead of pork belly (liempo).
  • Immune System Boost. Tamarind (sampalok) and tomatoes provide a healthy dose of Vitamin C, which protects the body and promotes skin repair.
  • Heart-Healthy. Vegetables Ingredients like kangkong (water spinach) and sitaw (string beans) contain high dietary fiber, which helps lower cholesterol and regulates blood sugar.
  • Digestive Aid. Tamarind naturally contains tartaric acid, which stimulates digestive enzymes and can soothe stomach discomfort.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. pork belly sliced into cubes
  • 2 to 3 cups kangkong leaves
  • 3 pieces long green chili pepper siling pansigang
  • 2 pieces tomato quartered
  • 1 cup sliced daikon radish labanos
  • 10 to 12 pieces snake beans cut in 2 inch length pieces
  • 1 piece onion quartered
  • 15 pieces okra
  • 2 pieces taro peeled and cut in half (gabi)
  • 8 cups water
  • 2 packs Knorr Sinigang sa Sampaloc Mix Original (20 gram pack)
  • 3 tablespoons fish sauce patis
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
